HandMade Comunity

handmade-community.org

This name represents a collective space for artisans and small businesses focused on handmade motorcycles and parts

It emphasizes community, collaboration, and authenticity

Site Purpose

The site provides a hub for artisans and entrepreneurs to showcase their handmade products

It offers a member directory, event listings, and a membership application form.

Visitors can discover photo galery, workshops, local events, and connect with artisans directly.
